How Palmetto Pantry Started

Palmetto Pantry Co. started with a simple idea: fresh, local food should be easier to fit into a busy schedule. South Carolina has plenty of great farms, but not everyone has time to shop for ingredients and plan meals every week.

That's why we created a delivery service for meal kits and specialty groceries from around the state. Based in Columbia, our goal is to make everyday meals more convenient while helping support South Carolina farmers.
